CodePlans / Reviews

Review the direction before the work starts

Code owners, architects and engineering managers review plans and specs at a pinned version, discuss them in context, and see only what is theirs to decide. Small teams can leave it all optional.

Responsibilities

Who reviews what comes from who owns what

Org roles decide what someone may do. Responsibilities decide who is asked: engineering managers and architects (optionally for an area) per product, and code owners per asset. They route reviews, triage and notifications; they never grant permissions.

Each product picks a workflow: Open (the default; reviews are optional) or Guided (reviewers are suggested from responsibilities, and activating unapproved work asks for confirmation).

Plan review

Review the plan before effort is spent

A PR review comes after the work is done. A plan review lets code owners stop a misdirected change before any tokens or people are spent on it. Suggested reviewers come from the plan’s target assets and the product’s responsibilities.

  • Approvals are pinned to a revision. Changing the plan’s scope, specs or description asks reviewers to look again.
  • Agents can request reviews and comment, but only people approve.

Spec review

Approve a version, not a moving target

Reviewers approve, request changes or comment on a specific spec version. A later revision marks earlier approvals outdated, and approvers and plan owners are told. Required reviewers must approve before the review completes.

Discussion

Feedback anchored to the text

Select text in a spec to comment on it, ask a question or suggest a change. Threads can be resolved, @mentions notify people, and anchors survive later revisions (or say when the quoted text changed). Viewers can comment too.
