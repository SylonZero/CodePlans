What needs you, and why

My Work turns responsibilities into a personal inbox. Notifications reach people in-app, by email and in Slack, with admin controls that stay simple for small teams.

My Work

An inbox shaped by your responsibilities

Needs you lists what is blocked on you, each item starting with a verb and saying why it is yours (for example code owner · API Gateway). In flight groups your tasks by plan with their specs, and Watching shows changes to things you are responsible for.

If you hold several responsibilities, View as switches between Developer, Code owner, Architect and Eng manager lenses.

Lenses

Each role gets its own panels

Engineering managers see plans at risk, release readiness and work merged but not shipped. Architects see reviews in their area. Code owners see their assets and capabilities delivered against an older spec.

Evidence gaps ask the person who can fix the record: a completed plan with no PR, a release asset with no version stamp, a feature never graduated.

The bell

Notifications with a reason

The bell shows unread notifications. Each says why you got it, and nobody is told about their own actions. Opening one marks it read; items can be marked done or snoozed from My Work.

Email & Slack

Delivered where your team already is

Admins connect Resend (an API key and a verified sender) and a Slack incoming webhook, then choose which events notify in-app, by email and in Slack. Test sends show the provider’s answer, and keys are encrypted at rest.

  • Required events (review requests, changes requested, mentions) always reach people in-app.
  • Sends go through an outbox with retries and backoff, visible under Recent deliveries.

One template, two channels

Email explains why you were told and links to your settings. Slack gets one post per event, worded for the whole channel.

Your settings

Turn email down, mute what you don’t need

Anyone can turn off all email or email for single events, which only ever narrows what admins turned on. Mute on a product or asset page stops notifications about it, except review requests and mentions.
